Target Settings

The Ride has exactly three adjustments. Saddle fore/aft isn't one of them — the frame's seat tube is fixed at 73.5° (K), so raising or lowering the saddle moves height and setback together, as one slide. We solve for the height your fit calls for, then show the setback you'll actually land on.
