Target Settings

Height, stack and reach ride on bolts with a fixed track — tape-measure from BB centre and you'll land exactly on target. The saddle's fore/aft clamp and tilt bolt have no printed scale, so treat those two as estimates: set roughly, ride a lap, nudge to feel.

Crank Length — Adjustable Crank Arms

Bearclaw-style arm, 5 pedal-hole positions. Pick the hole closest to your fit's crank length.
